# Evaluation Metrics for Retrieval Quality in RAG Systems

## Summary

Context precision measures the percentage of retrieved documents that are actually relevant to the query. [S3] Context recall measures whether retrieval captured all relevant documents available in the knowledge base. [S3] NDCG@K and Precision@K evaluate whether the most relevant documents appear near the top of the results. [S3]

## Limitations

Traditional retrieval indicators such as Recall and Precision cannot fully capture the nuances of RAG retrieval. [S1]
