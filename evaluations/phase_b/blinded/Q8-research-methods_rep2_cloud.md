# Metrics for assessing retrieval quality in RAG systems

## Summary

Context precision measures the percentage of retrieved documents that are relevant to the query. [S3] Context recall measures whether retrieval captured all relevant documents available in the knowledge base. [S3] NDCG@K and Precision@K are retrieval ranking metrics for evaluating whether the most relevant documents appear near the top of the results. [S3]

## Other retrieval-evaluation approaches

Retrieval evaluation can include recall@k with ground truth or manual or LLM-judged relevance scoring of retrieved context. [S4]

## Limitations

Traditional retrieval indicators such as recall and precision cannot fully capture the nuances of RAG retrieval systems. [S1]
