# Why Large Language Models Sometimes Produce Confident but False Statements

## Summary

Factually incorrect results can appear comprehensive, professional, and plausible, making them difficult for people to detect. [S4] Some reasoning failures arise because LLMs lack fundamental cognitive abilities that humans possess, leading to errors humans typically avoid. [S3]

## How false statements can seem credible

LLMs’ ability to generate human-like text can lead people to falsely credit them with other human capabilities, resulting in misapplications of the technology. [S2]
